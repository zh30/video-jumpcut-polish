# High-Exposure Video Copywriting & SEO Playbook

Use this playbook in Step 6 to extract real content signals from the edited
video and generate high-CTR titles, SEO-rich descriptions, hashtags, Show Notes,
and engagement-driving pinned comments across **抖音、小红书、微信视频号、B站、YouTube**.

---

## 1. Extract the Video's Signal Brief First (Never Write Generic Copy!)

Before drafting a single title, extract 5 concrete facts directly from what the
speaker actually said in the edited video:
1. **Core Thesis / Takeaway**: the single most valuable conclusion or insight.
2. **Concrete Numbers & Proof**: exact prices, hours, counts, timeframes, before/after contrasts (e.g., `84元`, `8小时`, `48小时`, `3个习惯`, `半年`).
3. **Target Audience & Pain Point**: who hurts from this problem (e.g., `戴入耳式耳道难受的人`, `30岁职场内耗`, `新手做播客`).
4. **3-Tier SEO Keyword Matrix**:
   - **Core (1–2 main terms)**: high-volume entity or topic (`AirBuds Lite`, `个人播客`, `专注力`).
   - **Long-Tail / Scene (2–3 terms)**: audience + scenario (`百元半入耳蓝牙耳机`, `上班通勤耳机`, `普通人一人公司`).
   - **Question / Search-Intent (1–2 terms)**: how users actually search (`半入耳蓝牙耳机怎么选`, `百元耳机值不值得买`).
5. **Golden Quotes (2–3 verbatim memorable lines)** from the speaker for Show Notes / intros.

---

## 2. The #1 CTR Rule: Cover ↔ Title Complementarity (互补，绝不重复)

- **Never copy the exact same phrase onto both the cover and the title.**
- **Cover (`3–6 words`)** carries **visual shock, bold number, or curiosity gap** (e.g., `不到百元!`, `别再假努力了`, `白忙三年？`).
- **Title** carries **rational search keywords + audience pain point + concrete value promise** (e.g., `戴入耳式耳道难受？百元半入耳蓝牙耳机实测：通勤8小时还有电`).
- Together, Cover + Title deliver **2x information gain** in 0.5 seconds.
- **Anti-Clickbait Rule**: Any curiosity gap opened by the cover/title MUST be honestly answered in the video (especially within the first 30 seconds) or retention collapses and algorithms kill reach.

---

## 3. Proven High-CTR Title Formulas (Generate 3 Distinct Angles per Platform)

Always write **3 candidate titles** covering 3 distinct psychological angles so
the creator can pick or A/B test:
1. **Pain Point / Contrast (痛点反差型)**:
   - `[痛点人群/反常识冲突] + [核心关键词] + [结果/悬念]`
   - Talking-head: `越努力越内耗？我花3年才想通的底层原因`
   - Unboxing: `戴入耳式耳道胀痛？自费试两款百元半入耳我留了它`
2. **Quantified Number / Value (数字干货型)**:
   - `[具体数字/成本] + [核心关键词] + [量化收益]`
   - Podcast: `从0做个人播客半年：总结出3条普通人最容易踩的坑`
   - Unboxing: `不到百元买48h续航？AirBuds Lite 半入耳真实上手`
3. **Audience Identity / Search-Intent (精准搜索共鸣型)**:
   - `[身份标签/搜索问句] + [核心关键词] + [真实结论]`
   - Talking-head: `给95后打工人的反焦虑指南：停止精神内耗的2个动作`
   - Unboxing: `百元半入耳蓝牙耳机怎么选？安卓+iPhone双机实测`

## 4. Platform-Specific Limits, Truncation Fold & Style Rules

Core SEO keywords **MUST appear BEFORE the truncation fold**:

| Platform | Safe Title / Lead Length (Before Fold) | Tone & Structure | Hashtag / Tag Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **小红书** | Title **≤ 20 字** (core keyword in first **10 字**; feed truncates at ~15–18 字) | Authentic KOC tone (zero ad-speak), scannable short lines + clean Emoji bullets, rich search keywords in first 2 lines | **3–5 hashtags**: 1 broad category + 2–3 long-tail scene/question tags + 1 brand/series tag |
| **抖音** | First **20–24 字** visible before "展开" fold | Punchy hook/conflict right at char 1, short rhythmic copy, strong comment prompt | **2–3 precise hashtags** strictly matching video vertical (never hijack unrelated trends) |
| **微信视频号** | First **15–25 字** visible in feed | Trustworthy, warm, share-worthy to friends/colleagues; clear practical value or relatable life insight | **3–4 tags** + warm conversational CTA encouraging likes/shares |
| **B 站** | **18–35 字** (max ~40 字, crisp community feel) | Honest, geeky/sincere, zero cheap clickbait; description first 2 lines weave core keywords + **full `00:00` chapter timestamps** + mentioned tools/books | **5–8 tags** + exact sub-category (`分区`) |
| **YouTube** | **≤ 28 中文字 / ≤ 60 ASCII chars** (mobile safe) | Clear search-first value promise; first 2 lines natural SEO summary + **`00:00` chapters** + resource links | 3–5 concise tags |

## 5. Genre-Specific Description Templates

### A. Solo Talking-Head (单人口播 / 干货) — PAS + Key Takeaways
- **Lines 1–2**: Hook + core keyword summary (answers "why watch?").
- **Body**: 3 crisp bullet takeaways (what the viewer walks away with).
- **Close**: Authentic conversational question (not stiff "请一键三连").

### B. Video Podcast / Monologue (个人播客 / 深度访谈) — Show Notes Format
- **Lines 1–2**: Episode core question + strongest insight (with SEO keywords).
- **Timestamps (`00:00`)**: Complete navigable chapter outline (essential for Bilibili/YouTube/Xiaoyuzhou).
- **Golden Quotes (`本期金句`)**: 2–3 verbatim punchy sentences from the speaker.
- **Mentioned Resources (`本期提及`)**: Books, apps, gear, or concepts mentioned (omit if none).

### C. Product Unboxing / Review (开箱 / 实物测评) — Honest KOC Pros & Cons
- **Lines 1–2**: Buying criteria + exact product model + real paid price.
- **Structured Verdict**: `✅ 优点` (2–3 concrete facts) / `⚠️ 客观不足` (honest drawbacks build massive trust!) / `🎯 适合谁` (target buyer).

---

## 6. Pinned Comment (置顶评论 — Double Engine for Engagement + SEO)

Every platform gets a tailored **Pinned Comment** doing two jobs:
1. **Low-friction binary or opinion question** ("你平时更喜欢 A 还是 B？评论区聊聊") to spark fast early replies in the Golden 1st Hour.
2. **Secondary SEO reinforcement**: naturally weave 1–2 long-tail search keywords that didn't fit cleanly in the title (platforms index pinned comments for search!).

---

## 7. De-AI-ifying & Compliance Guardrails

- **Sound like the real speaker**: mirror their spoken vocabulary and candid attitude (e.g., if they honestly admit "塑料感十足", keep that authenticity in the copy!).
- **Zero fake hype**: ban empty AI clichés (`震撼来袭`, `不容错过`, `干货满满`, `家人们谁懂啊` unless it matches the speaker's persona) and Advertising Law extreme words (`全网最低`, `第一`, `绝对`, `顶级`, `根治`).
- **Zero off-platform引流**: never put WeChat/QQ/QR/external-link bait in titles, copy, or pinned comments.
